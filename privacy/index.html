⚖️ LegalWithDev — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

1. What is stored

On our website, your chat history is never stored on the server. The conversation stays only in your browser's memory (RAM) and is gone when you close the page.

On Telegram / WhatsApp, your recent messages stay only in the server's temporary memory (RAM) to remember the conversation context. This data is not saved to disk and is cleared when the server restarts.

2. Where your data goes

When you ask a question, the message reaches our server over an HTTPS (encrypted) connection and is sent to the Google Gemini API to generate the AI answer.

As per Google's Unpaid (Free) Tier terms: on the free tier, Google may use your submitted content and AI responses to improve its products and machine learning, and for quality purposes human reviewers may also read this data (Google displays this data separated from your account/API key). That is why Google itself says: "Do not submit sensitive, confidential, or personal information to the Unpaid Services".

🔒 So our request: please do not share your sensitive personal information in the chat — name, case numbers, documents, address, Aadhaar. Ask your question in general terms (for example: "My landlord is not returning my deposit, what can I do?").

We do not sell your data to anyone, and we do not use it for AI training ourselves.

3. Rate limiting

To keep the service free for everyone, each user/IP has a limited number of messages per hour (to prevent abuse/spam).

4. What we do NOT do

  • Ask for or store your name, phone, address or case details
  • Sell data or use it for advertising
  • Read back your chat history (it is never stored in the first place)

5. Contact & Disclaimer

LegalWithDev is an AI legal-information assistant — not an advocate. It provides general legal information, not legal advice. For your case, please consult a qualified Advocate/Lawyer. Free legal aid: nalsa.gov.in · 15100.
